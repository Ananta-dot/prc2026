Built by pipeline/build.py from
  five runs of stage2.py --stages 3 --lr 0.05 on the final ranking set, trained on all of 2025:
    seed 7 (--rounds 2681,300,851), seed 11 (--rounds 2420,300,300), seeds 23, 31 and 47 (--rounds 2550,300,575);
  and, for the January and July flights, the three runs on the leaderboard ranking set behind v6 (weight 3 to 5).
Rules of pipeline/posthoc.py applied to every run before averaging (rows whose plain average is above 4 h come from the seed-7 run):
  pairing window for matched departures; flight plans linked from the arrival record for unmatched ones (778 late flights:
  679 ordinary, 28 near-schedule, 71 with a partly updated plan); Rome summer-time records (8 flights);
  Rome flight numbers that are always schedule-default (about 1,070 flights).
Against the first final file (three runs, no rules): RMS change about 47 s; about 5,500 predictions move by more than a minute, 260 by more than ten.
No matched departure is predicted outside its pairing window any more (124 were in the first final file).
